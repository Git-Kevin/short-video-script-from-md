# Style Presets

Use these presets when the user asks for a specific tone or when you need a default.

## Knowledge Breakdown

- Best for: tutorials, AI tools, business insight, methodology
- Hook pattern: `You think X matters most, but the real driver is Y`
- Voice: clear, confident, teacher-like, but conversational
- Pace: medium-fast
- Shot direction: talking head + keyword overlays + simple B-roll

## Counterintuitive Take

- Best for: opinion pieces, trend analysis, hot takes
- Hook pattern: `Most people have this backwards`
- Voice: assertive, punchy
- Pace: fast
- Shot direction: fast cuts, bold on-screen text, contrast examples

## Story Case Study

- Best for: founder stories, company cases, user stories
- Hook pattern: `They only wanted to solve one small problem, and it turned into...`
- Voice: narrative, visual, slightly dramatic
- Pace: medium
- Shot direction: chapter cards, screenshots, timeline visuals

## Checklist Advice

- Best for: productivity, operations, creator advice, tactical lists
- Hook pattern: `If you want to do X well, get these 3 things right first`
- Voice: direct and actionable
- Pace: medium-fast
- Shot direction: numbered bullets, gesture cuts, demonstration inserts

## Emotional Resonance

- Best for: personal growth, creator mindset, empathy-driven content
- Hook pattern: `A lot of people are stuck, not because they are lazy, but because...`
- Voice: warm, reflective
- Pace: medium-slow
- Shot direction: close-up, pause points, softer edits

## Product Seeding

- Best for: product introduction, brand storytelling, conversion content
- Hook pattern: `If this scenario sounds familiar, this approach is worth trying`
- Voice: credible, non-hype, benefit-led
- Pace: medium
- Shot direction: use-case demo, before/after, interface close-ups

## Personal IP

- Best for: founder account, expert account, creator account
- Hook pattern: `One thing I have been repeatedly validating lately is...`
- Voice: first-person, opinionated, grounded in experience
- Pace: medium
- Shot direction: direct-to-camera, working scenes, annotated screenshots

## Adaptation Rules

- Keep creator-style requests at the level of rhythm, structure, and energy.
- Do not mimic trademarked slogans or signature lines.
- If the source content is dry, inject energy through framing and pacing, not by changing the facts.
